# Sources

Everything in this curriculum was built and cross-checked against the official
Division B rules and a set of real past Crime Busters exams with answer keys, plus
prior-year coaching decks. **None of those files are in this repo** — the exams
and decks are copyrighted by the schools and individuals who wrote them, and this
repo is public. They are kept locally in a git-ignored `raw/` folder. This page
lists them so it's clear where the content came from and what each one
contributed.

Companion doc: [`Test_Coverage_Map.md`](Test_Coverage_Map.md) maps each test topic
to where it's taught and practiced.

## Finding these tests yourself

Science Olympiad invitational tests and keys are posted by the hosting schools
and on test-exchange sites (search the invitational name + "Crime Busters Division
B"). The SSSS series is shared through the SciOly forums and subreddit. Use tests
your team has **not** seen for the three practice-test sessions.
