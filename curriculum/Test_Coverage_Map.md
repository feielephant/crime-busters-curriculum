# Test Coverage Map

An audit of the curriculum against the official 2025 Division B rules and every
real invitational test used as a reference during development (CVI 2026, OVI 2026,
Pembroke Hill, the Archimedean/USC/Purdue/BullSO bundle, "Crime Busters B" USC
2026, and the SSSS 2017 / 2022 / 2023 / 2024 sets).

For each topic: where it's **taught** (session / Knowledge Document chapter) and
where it's **practiced** (quiz / homework). ✅ = fully covered · ⚠️ = partially
covered · ➕ = added in the coverage pass (Homework 13–14).

## The short answer

**Every topic that appears on the tests is taught somewhere in the curriculum,
and the great majority is also drilled in a quiz or homework.** The coverage pass
added **Homework 13 and 14** to close the ⚠️/➕ gaps — mostly loose "general
knowledge / junior detective" short-answer items (flame-test colors, PCR stages,
the blood-spatter information list, minutiae identification, powder uses, natural
polymers, empirical formulas, and a few advanced-chemistry stretch questions from
the harder SSSS tests).

## A note on scope

The **official 2025 rules** are narrower than what many invitationals actually
test. The rules ask only for:

- animal / vegetable / synthetic fibers — **not** specific fiber names
- human / dog / cat hair — **not** guard hair etc.
- whorl / loop / arch — **not** the full IAFIS 8-pattern set
- **no calculations** for spatter or tracks
- the 7 listed plastics — **not** PC
- the 13 listed solids, 6 liquids, 6 metals

Invitationals routinely go past all of that (specific fibers, IAFIS patterns, the
spatter-angle calculation, polycarbonate, off-list powders like Epsom salt and
sodium acetate). This curriculum teaches the **invitational-level** version so the
team is ready for the hardest tests they'll see — but the coach should know which
parts are "core" (always tested) and which are "invitational stretch," and the
Knowledge Document flags this per topic.
