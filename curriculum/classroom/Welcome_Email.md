**What Session 1 actually is**

Our very first practice (Sept 17) is a season kickoff, not science yet: class rules, safety rules, how the event and scoring work, and a walkthrough of what a real test looks like. Every kid goes home that day with a **home practice kit** (food-grade powders + supplies for practicing at home) — we'll also send home a **Home Lab Safety Agreement** that needs a parent signature before any home lab work starts.

**How homework works**

Starting with Session 2, each practice ends with a take-home assignment tied to that day's topic. Due 2 days before the next practice. The exact filename to look for each week is included on the calendar event and the week's syllabus.

**The season at a glance**

- **Session 1:** Orientation & event overview (rules, safety, scoring, kit handout — see above)
- **Session 2:** Powders 101 — the testing workflow and the chemistry behind it (a longer session)
- **Session 3:** What the Judges Actually Score — how the written analysis gets graded
- **Sessions 4–6:** Mixtures & equations, liquids & pH, metals
- **Practice Test 1**, then **Sessions 7–10:** hair & DNA, fibers & chromatography, plastics, blood
- **Practice Test 2**, then **Sessions 11–12:** blood spatter & glass, soil/impressions/prints
- **Practice Test 3** — final timed dry run before competition

Bring safety goggles and an apron/old clothes every week — most sessions involve a hands-on lab station. Bring a non-graphing calculator for Session 2 (Powders 101), Session 4 (Mixtures & Equations), Session 6 (Metals), Session 10 (Blood), and all three practice tests.
