# Homework 1: What the Judges Actually Score

*Paper track. Original scenario, invitational format. Do this on your own.*

---

## The Scene

At a weekend robotics tournament, the club's cash bag (about $600 in
registration fees) disappears from the check-in table between 8:15 and 8:40 a.m.
The table stands on a strip of exposed dirt just inside the gym's side door.

Investigators recover: a smear of **white powder** on the table edge that
**turns iodine blue-black** and does **not** fizz with acid; two **short white
animal hairs** on the cash bag strap; a **synthetic (polyester) fiber**, navy
blue, caught on the zipper; a small crumb of **dark, fine, iron-rich reddish
soil**; and a **partial fingerprint (tented arch)** on the metal clasp.

## The Suspects

- **Reese** — runs the snack table (which has flour, powdered sugar, and corn
  starch out for a baking demo). Wears a navy cotton club shirt. Has a white
  cat. Was refilling the coffee urn at 8:20 (two witnesses). Fingerprints: ulnar
  loop, ulnar loop.
- **Sky** — the tournament's setup volunteer; had a key to the cash bag lock.
  Wears a navy **polyester** windbreaker. No pets. "Checking the field alone"
  from 8:10 to 8:45. Fingerprints: tented arch, radial loop.
- **Devon** — maintains the school garden; keeps a bin of **reddish iron-rich
  potting soil** by the gym's side door. Wears a gray fleece. Has a white dog.
  Fingerprints: plain whorl, ulnar loop.
- **Bailey** — was denied a refund last week and complained loudly to the club
  treasurer. Runs the 3-D-printing booth (lots of PLA plastic filament, no
  powders). Wears a green wool sweater. No pets. Fingerprints: plain arch,
  tented arch.

## Problems

**1. (5 pts)** Complete the evidence grid. For each item: which suspect(s) does
it point to, and is it a **strong** (unusual/specific) or **common** match?

| Evidence | Points to | Strong or common? |
|---|---|---|
| White powder — iodine-positive, no fizz | | |
| Short white animal hair | | |
| Navy polyester fiber | | |
| Reddish iron-rich soil | | |
| Tented arch fingerprint | | |

**2. (2 pts)** Which suspect has the strongest **motive**? Do the physical
evidence and that suspect line up? What does that tell you?

**3. (10 pts)** Write a full analysis: name the culprit; give a motive; list the
evidence against them, each with a reason; give a timeline note; and clear each
of the other three suspects in a sentence.

**4. (1 pt)** The white powder is iodine-positive and doesn't fizz. Name a powder
it could be, with its formula.

**5. (2 pts)** Explain why "the tented arch fingerprint matches Sky and Bailey"
is *class* evidence, not proof.

---

## Answer Key

**1.**

| Evidence | Points to | Strong/common |
|---|---|---|
| White powder, iodine +, no fizz | Reese (has corn starch / flour at the snack table) | **strong** — Bailey has no powders; a starch powder is specific |
| White animal hair | Reese (cat) or Devon (dog) — need species ID to split | common |
| Navy polyester fiber | Sky (polyester windbreaker) — Reese's navy shirt is *cotton*, not polyester | **strong** — the fiber class rules Reese out for this item |
| Reddish iron-rich soil | Devon (reddish iron-rich potting soil bin by the side door) | **strong** — distinctive color/composition |
| Tented arch print | Sky or Bailey (both have a tented arch); excludes Reese and Devon | class evidence |

**2.** Bailey has the strongest motive (denied a refund, complained). But the
physical evidence doesn't point to Bailey — no powder (Bailey has only plastic
filament), a wool sweater (not the polyester fiber), no pet (the animal hair).
The best motive belongs to a suspect the evidence clears. Say that explicitly.

**3.** Model answer:
- **Culprit: Sky.** Motive: had a key and the only unsupervised window (8:10–8:45,
  "checking the field alone" — no witness).
- Evidence: navy **polyester** fiber on the zipper matches Sky's polyester
  windbreaker (Reese's navy shirt is cotton); **tented arch** print on the clasp
  is consistent with Sky and excludes Reese and Devon; had the **key** and **no
  alibi**.
- Timeline: Sky alone 8:10–8:45, covering the whole theft window; Reese was
  refilling coffee at 8:20 with two witnesses.
- Clear the others:
  - **Reese** — the iodine-positive powder and a white pet match, but the fiber
    is polyester (Reese wears cotton), and Reese has a witnessed alibi at 8:20.
  - **Devon** — the reddish soil matches Devon's potting-soil bin, and Devon has
    a white pet, but there's no powder link, no fiber match, and the tented arch
    print excludes Devon (whorl/loop). The soil could be secondary transfer — the
    bin is right by the door everyone used.
  - **Bailey** — strongest motive but **no** matching physical evidence (plastic
    filament not powder, wool not polyester, no pet). Motive without evidence is
    not proof.

**4.** Corn starch — (C₆H₁₀O₅)ₙ. (Flour also acceptable.)

**5.** Lots of people have a tented arch (~a few percent of prints, but that's
still millions of people), and a partial print can't be matched on minutiae. It
narrows the field (rules out anyone without a tented arch) but doesn't identify
one person the way a full minutiae match or DNA would.
