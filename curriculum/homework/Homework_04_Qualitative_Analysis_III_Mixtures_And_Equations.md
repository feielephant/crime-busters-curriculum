## Part C: Worked example — copy the method (6 pts)

**Solved for you:** 12 g of baking soda (NaHCO₃, 84 g/mol) reacts with excess
vinegar. How many grams of CO₂ (44 g/mol) are produced?
`NaHCO₃ + CH₃COOH → NaC₂H₃O₂ + H₂O + CO₂`
- moles NaHCO₃ = 12 ÷ 84 = 0.143 mol
- ratio NaHCO₃ : CO₂ is 1 : 1 → 0.143 mol CO₂
- mass CO₂ = 0.143 × 44 = **6.3 g**

**Now you do it:** 21 g of baking soda reacts with excess vinegar. How many grams
of CO₂ are produced? Show every step.

**Part D:**
